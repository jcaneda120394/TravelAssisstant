## Your Travel Companion Anywhere in the World

Plan your trip. Discover places. Find the best way to get there.  
Manage travel essentials. Ask your AI travel assistant.

## 1. What is TravelAssistant?

TravelAssistant brings the important parts of traveling into one app.

- Discover attractions, food, and hotels nearby
- Get directions and compare travel modes
- Check weather and convert currencies
- Browse trip suggestions and save plans (when signed in)
- Ask an AI travel assistant about places, routes, and budgets
- Find eSIM offers and emergency help near you
- Save favorites and build a simple offline pack

<p class="caption">① Choose your city · ② Plan trips & suggestions · ③ Ask AI · ④ Open Explore</p>

## 13. Directions & transport

1. Start from **Your location** (or pick another origin)
2. Search for a destination
3. Choose a mode: **Best, Drive, Walk, Cycle** (and more when available)
4. Review the route on the map and the step list

<p class="note">Driving, walking, and cycling use live road data when available. Bus, train, rideshare, and flight options may be estimated — not live schedules or ticket prices.</p>

## 14. AI Travel Assistant

Ask TravelAssistant questions while planning or traveling.

<p class="caption">① Modes · ② Assistant reply · ③ Type or speak</p>

**Modes:** ask · explore · planner · navigator · emergency · budget

Try asking:

- “What should we do this afternoon?”
- “Where should we eat nearby?”
- “How do I get to the nearest museum?”
- “Is the weather good for outdoor plans?”

You can type or use the microphone. The assistant focuses on travel topics only.

## 22. Offline travel

On **Profile**, signed-in travelers can **Build offline pack**.

What is cached today:

- Your saved trips (summary)
- Emergency numbers snapshot

This is not a full offline map. An offline banner appears when your connection drops.

## Tips for a great first trip

1. Set your **display currency** on Home.
2. **Choose city** before Explore or AI.
3. Browse as a guest, then **sign in** to save.
4. Confirm prices, hours, and emergency numbers on site.
5. Ask AI for ideas — then verify routes on Directions.
